Why members join and stay

A club built for getting vulnerable, scrappy, and unstoppable…on your terms.

Speaking opportunities large and small available weekly

Structured speaking roles for every skill level, from your first Ice Breaker to competition-ready contest speeches.

Supportive, actionable feedback

Every speaker gets specific, actionable evaluations — the fastest way we know to actually improve.

Expand as a leader

Contribute to our success through running a meeting, managing a project, or serving as a club officer. We can't think of a better way to hone the very skills that deliver personally and professionally.

Fun vibes

Our club doesn't ask you to fit a mold. We ask only that you show up and grow in whatever way is authentic to you. What we do not embrace: rigid outdated frameworks, policies which inhibit member growth, and cultures mistaking familiarity for value.

About the Club

The old playbook died with the dinosaurs…so we wrote our own!

Northeast Seattle Toastmasters, Club 1161, was chartered in 1952 — but the club that meets today is diverse, innovative, and dynamic, and it's grown into one of the largest and fastest-growing clubs on the West Coast. What hasn't changed is the community: since 1952 we've helped hundreds of members — students, engineers, executives, career-changers, retirees, entrepreneurs, authors, and other creators — get more comfortable and more capable in front of a room, and build real relationships along the way.

Our Mission

We provide a supportive, positive environment built around your individual goals and projects — not a one-size-fits-all curriculum — so every member can develop communication and leadership skills that fit their own path to self-confidence and growth.

Community & Relationships

Members come from every background and career stage — preparing for a big presentation, working toward a leadership title, or just wanting to think faster on their feet. The friendships and support built along the way are a big part of why people stay.

Growing Fast

We're proud to be one of the largest, and by far the fastest-growing, clubs on the West Coast — a lively, ever-evolving mix of members, backgrounds, and meeting formats.

Meeting Roles & What to Expect (guide) →

Your first meeting

Sit in and see the format

A member greets you at the door and explains the meeting roles. You're welcome to just observe.

When you're ready

Become an official member

Download and complete the New Member Application, then email it with your dues to our officers.

Weeks 1–2

Get a mentor and pick a Pathway

New members are paired with a mentor and choose a Pathways learning track based on their goals.

Weeks 3–6

Give your Ice Breaker speech

Your first prepared speech introduces you to the club — short, and supportive.

Months 2–6

Build momentum

Take on a meeting role, give 2–3 more speeches, and get comfortable with structured feedback.

See Member Spotlights →

6+ months

Step into leadership

Run for a club officer role, mentor the next new guest, or compete at an Area speech contest.
